**Question:** which post-capture pipeline gives the sharpest splat, and is Metashape's
edge its bundle adjustment (replicable free with COLMAP) or something more?

## What to compare, in order

1. **1 vs 3 (direct vs Metashape)** — the gap being investigated. Confirm the
   sharpness difference you saw on the 764-shot scene reproduces here.
2. **4 vs 3 (BA+fusion vs Metashape)** — the decision. Run 4 is the best free
   pipeline; if it matches 3, COLMAP BA becomes the default export and Metashape
   is reserved for hero captures. If it visibly trails, Metashape's remaining edge
   is per-group distortion calibration → next step is an OPENCV-model
   self-calibration pass in the COLMAP leg.
3. **4 vs 2 (fused seeds vs tie-point seeds, same poses)** — isolates seed density.
   Expect 4 to be more complete in textureless areas (bare walls, floors).
4. **4 vs 1 (BA poses vs raw VIO, similar seeds)** — isolates registration.
   Sharpness difference here ≈ what BA alone buys.

## What to look for

- **Sharpness:** readable texture (labels, wood grain, fabric) at ~1–2 m. Misregistration
  looks like uniform motion blur; defocus looks the same everywhere — distinguish them.
- **Edges:** crisp object silhouettes vs fuzzy double-edges (pose error) on door frames,
  table edges, monitor bezels.
- **Floaters:** drifting blobs in free space, especially near depth discontinuities.
- **Textureless coverage:** holes or thin spots on bare walls/floor (seed-density signal).
- **Color consistency:** patchy brightness/tint between view directions (AWB/exposure drift).
